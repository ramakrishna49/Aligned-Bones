Privacy Policy

How information entered on the Aligned Bones website is handled and how website enquiries work.

Updated November 2026 Aligned Bones

This page explains how information entered on the Aligned Bones website moves through the current website experience.

01

Information you provide

Depending on the form you use, you may enter your name, phone number, email address, age, preferred consultation, doctor preference, preferred appointment date or time, enquiry type, and a message about your concern.

Only include details needed for your enquiry. Please do not use a website form to send medical reports or information you do not want to share through WhatsApp.

02

How our forms work

The appointment, contact and newsletter forms prepare a message in WhatsApp. The website opens WhatsApp with the details you entered; it does not send the message automatically. You review it and choose whether to press Send.

If you send the message, it is shared with the Aligned Bones WhatsApp contact and processed through WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own Privacy Policy applies to your use of its service. An appointment form is a request; the clinic must contact you to confirm it.

03

Third-party services

Some pages load fonts and icons from external providers, and contact or booking actions may open WhatsApp. When your browser connects to those services, their own terms and privacy information apply to that connection. Aligned Bones does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

04

Website storage

The current website does not provide user accounts or online payments, and its forms do not submit details to a built-in website database. Form details remain in the page until you leave or clear them, and are included in the WhatsApp message only when you choose to open the contact flow.

The website code does not currently include an analytics or advertising tracker. Your browser and hosting provider may process technical information needed to load and secure a website.

05

Your choices

  • You can leave any form before opening WhatsApp.
  • You can review or edit the prepared message before sending it.
  • For questions about information already sent to the clinic, call and identify the message or request.
06

Contact us

For a question about this website policy, call Aligned Bones at +91 82963 25500.
